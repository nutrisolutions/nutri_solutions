## What end-to-end development means at Nutri Solutions
Hiring separate vendors for design, development, hosting and support creates gaps: decisions get lost between handovers, and nobody owns the result. With one team, the choices made in discovery carry straight through to the code, and the people who built your application are the same people who support it.
Every project we deliver goes through six stages:
- Discover: we agree goals, users and scope up front, so the estimate and timeline are grounded in what you actually need.
- Design: UI/UX for web and mobile, reviewed with you before development starts.
- Build: the web application, the Flutter mobile app and the APIs that connect them.
- Test: quality assurance before every release, not just at the end.
- Launch: deployment on AWS, set up to scale as usage grows.
- Support: updates, fixes and improvements after go-live.

## The technology behind them
All five applications share the same proven foundation: the MERN stack (MongoDB, Express, React and Node.js) for web applications and APIs, Flutter for cross-platform mobile apps on Android and iOS, and AWS for hosting, storage and scaling. Using one stack across projects means faster delivery, shared practices for security and performance, and simpler long-term maintenance for our clients.
## Is end-to-end development right for your project?
It is usually a good fit when:
- you need a web application and a mobile app that share the same data;
- you don't have an in-house engineering team, or yours is busy with core work;
- you want one accountable partner from planning to post-launch support;
- you are replacing spreadsheets, paper or disconnected tools with a single platform.
